# Kit name candidates (checked 2026-10-18, about 15:30 UTC)

Every name below was checked directly at the registries a few minutes before this file was
written: Verisign for `.com`, Identity Digital for `.io`, Google Registry for `.dev` and `.app`,
and `github.com/<name>` for the GitHub organisation name. Availability changes by the hour, so
register the chosen one soon after choosing.

## Free in .com, .io, .dev and .app, and free on GitHub

| Name | Where it comes from | Why it is easy to remember |
|---|---|---|
| **Trellano** | *trellis*, the frame a plant grows on | reads like Trello; each client's business grows on the same frame |
| **Stampano** | *stamp*: one stamp, many prints | says exactly what the kit does, the same in English and Spanish |
| **Loomano** | *loom*: one loom weaves many cloths | soft, two beats, no odd spelling |
| **Shapano** | *shape* | plain word, friendly ending |
| **Cimbrella** | Spanish *cimbra*, the mould that shapes an arch | echoes "umbrella", so people spell it right first time |
| **Zocalino** | Spanish *zócalo*, the plinth a building stands on | warm, Latin feel, fits the Spanish-speaking clients |
| **Trazaria** | Spanish *trazar*, to draw up a plan | flows well, sounds like a place |
| **Grundello** | *ground* | playful, two clear syllables |
| **Plintaro** | *plinth*, the base a column stands on | short and firm |
| **Sproutano** | *sprout*: a new business starting out | light, growth image |
| **Graftano** | *graft*: one root stock, many shoots | gardening image of "one kit, many clients" |

## What is taken

Every real word tried is registered in `.com`, and the memorable ones also in `.io` and `.dev`:

- English: foothold, groundwork, mainstay, linchpin, touchstone, heartwood, ballast, buttress,
  bulwark, foyer, mainsail, stronghold, keel, trellis, tapestry, meadow, pharos, parapet,
  courtyard, lodestone, plinth, lattice, loom, scaffold, framework, blueprint, bedrock, keystone,
  cornerstone, backbone, fingerpost, lamplight, longhouse, footbridge, gatehouse, waymark,
  hearthlight, osier, and about 150 compounds (kitwell, stackwell, basewell, everframe, truekit,
  steadykit, oakloft, fernloft, hearthloom, stampwell, homestamp, and so on).
- Other languages, all meaning foundation, frame, platform or kit: alusta, perusta, runko, kehys,
  jalusta (Finnish); themelio, krepis, pegma, harmos, tekton, techne, arche (Greek); compago,
  compages, fundamen, suggestus, pulpitum (Latin); andamio, cimbra, zocalo, basamento, alicerce,
  esteio (Spanish, Portuguese); telaio, ossatura, traliccio (Italian); kiban, dodai, honegumi,
  wakugumi, tsugite, kiwari, daiza (Japanese); neev, buniyaad, dhancha, saancha, aangan, manch
  (Hindi, Urdu); temel, zemin, iskele (Turkish); msingi (Swahili); rangka, landasan, tapak
  (Indonesian, Malay); balangkas, saligan (Tagalog); sylfaen (Welsh); kadro, ilaro (Esperanto);
  stomme, byggsats, grundsten, reisverk (Scandinavian); gicho, giban (Korean); manassa, haykal,
  qalab, tashtit (Arabic, Hebrew); tuapapa, kaupapa (Maori).
- Coined names with a common shape (-ora, -io, -ly, -ify, -ello on a common stem) are mostly
  registered too: vantora, lumera, kavio, framely, keelify, framello, kitello, basello, fundaro.

A real dictionary word in `.com` therefore means buying it from its current owner. Prices are not
visible from here; such names usually sell for thousands of dollars.
