# Life Timeline — Harshil

> Private. Plain-language forecast from the birth chart, September 2026 onward.
> Period dates are exact. Planet-movement and eclipse dates are approximate (give or take a week or two).
> Each month covers: **Work · Money · Official · Love · Family · Body · Mind · Do**.
> "Official" = paperwork, permissions, approvals, disputes. Where it says *hold*, hold. Where it says *sign*, sign.
> The periods travel with you: wherever you are living, the same months apply.

## 2026

### Rest of September 2026 — the pressure cooker
- **Work:** a question of authority is live (a manager, a role, your standing). Do the work, document it, say little.
- **Money:** one unexpected expense. Nothing big.
- **Official:** gather documents and see an adviser now — meetings with people in authority go better this month than next.
- **Love:** quiet. Loneliness sharpest in the evenings.
- **Family:** a father-related worry or conversation.
- **Body:** acidity, heat, a minor cut or bump. Sleep early.
- **Mind:** the "dead end" feeling is the planets talking, not the truth. No permanent decisions.

### October 2026 — two halves
Days 1–13 are the sharpest edge of the year. From the 13th a new current starts (it runs to 17 Mar 2027): unconventional routes, paperwork, foreign contacts, beating the odds.
- **Work:** 5–12 Oct — the argument you must not have, the resignation impulse, the driving scare. *Hold.* After the 13th: a contact, an idea, a route you hadn't considered, usually through a foreign colleague or online.
- **Money:** a delayed payment, a purchase reconsidered, an old money matter resurfaces (3 Oct → 14 Nov). Don't start a loan.
- **Official:** 24 Oct → 13 Nov forms get returned, portals glitch, a resubmission. Respond the same day, calmly. Begin the unconventional route this month.
- **Love:** someone from the past may message. Don't restart it.
- **Body:** fine after the 13th; mid-month fatigue.
- **Mind:** relief around the 13th–15th; a small lift in the last days of the month.
- **Do:** start the side income; list every option; talk to your employer.

### November 2026 — the hardest-feeling month of the year (planned, not permanent)
- **Work:** from ~10 Nov sharp emails, misunderstandings, an error in a document. Read everything twice. Pending things pause around the 11th.
- **Money:** tight from the 17th. No new spending.
- **Official:** silence, no replies. The silence is the pause, not the answer.
- **Love:** low. Don't judge your love life this month.
- **Family:** a call about a parent's health or a relative's demand.
- **Body:** throat, cold, sinus. Sleep.
- **Mind:** between ~20 Nov and ~10 Dec you will be convinced it's hopeless. It's written here in advance so you can recognise it. Do nothing irreversible.
- **Do:** routine; one weekly social thing; keep working the route quietly.

### December 2026 — the shift begins
Around the 5th a new 18-month cycle starts in your family-and-money house and your house of change.
- **Work:** stuck things move an inch from ~8 Dec. A reference or an assignment that helps later.
- **Money:** a family conversation about money or support; an income opportunity appears. Year-end gifts are your leak.
- **Official:** the unconventional route gives its first real signal — a reply, a lead, an eligibility you didn't know about. Keep it moving through the holidays.
- **Love:** quiet until the 15th, then invitations. Go.
- **Family:** a shift in the family's arrangements or expectations; support from an unexpected relative.
- **Body / Mind:** better after the 15th.
- **Do:** travel if you can. Sign nothing between 17 Nov and 15 Dec.

## Standing rules (apply every year)

### Calendar
- Best day to start anything: **Thursday morning** (second: Sunday).
- Best days each month: Moon in Taurus or Cancer. Worst: Moon in Gemini, Scorpio or Pisces.
- Best months: **mid-March → mid-June**. Worst: mid-June → mid-July, and **mid-November → mid-December**.
- Interviews and exams: late May → June, and late December → January.
- Love and money: mid-October → mid-November is your best stretch; mid-July → mid-September the flattest.
- Two stars to avoid for anything big (any panchang app shows the day's star): **Hasta** and **Shatabhisha**.
- Productive hours: mornings. Worst decisions: after 10 pm.

### The five things to avoid
1. Speculation, trading, quick-money schemes.
2. Lending to friends; business with friends before 2031.
3. Night shifts and alcohol.
4. Deciding in secret and announcing when done (at home).
5. Comfort spending and over-generosity — the leak.

### Stress signature
Stomach → silence → sweets → late-night scrolling → one sharp remark. Catch it at the stomach.

- [ ] Six months of expenses saved by end-2031
