# Phase 3: alerts

> **In plain words.** When System health finds a problem that lasts (a check failed twice in a
> row), the portal now emails the people an Owner names on the Settings page, once. At night,
> in the quiet hours the Owner sets, a warning waits and goes out in the morning with any
> others; a critical alert goes out at once. The new Alerts page lists what is open, and anyone
> allowed can press "I am on it" so the others know. When the check passes again, the alert
> closes by itself.

## What changed, and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **`@oltrem/alerts`**: the `oltrem_alerts` table (migration `0015`), two routes, the `alerts.release` job, three settings, two capabilities on bit plane 8 | Plan 4.4: platform alerts, notification routing, quiet windows; plan 5.1 names the recipients as a live setting |
| **The client template** raises a warning when a health problem opens and resolves it when it closes, and sends alerts through email with the plain `alerts.notice` template | The first source of alerts, and the one way out the kit has |
| **The Alerts page** in `@oltrem/screens`, its chip in the row and its line on Home | The alerts are seen and acknowledged from a phone |
| **The manifest gate** checks the module's test Worker | Every module's test configuration is checked against its manifest |

## Decisions taken while building

- **Email is handed in.** The module boundaries let a module depend only on the core and IAM,
  so the module takes a `send` function; the client template passes email's. A module of a
  client can raise an alert the same way.
- **Once per problem.** An alert is keyed by what raised it, and a unique index keeps one open
  row per key, so a problem that is raised every five minutes is emailed once and counted.
- **Held, then together.** A warning raised in the quiet hours is held; the job sends every held
  alert in one message when they end, and every alert email refused, so a missing sending
  address costs no alert. A critical one is never held.
- **Health problems are warnings.** A check that fails twice opens a warning; code that knows a
  problem is critical raises it as one.
- **"Sent" means handed to email.** Email holds a message while no provider is ready and sends it
  once one is; its own page shows what became of it.

## How it was verified

- The module's tests inside the Workers runtime, with the real settings reader: an alert with
  nobody named only listed; raised, emailed, raised again without a second email, resolved, and
  raised anew; held at 23:00 in Mexico City, a critical one sent at once, the held one sent at
  08:00 and not again; two refused by email, then sent together by the job; a bad time zone,
  quiet hours and recipients refused; the routes listing, acknowledging once with an audit row,
  a second acknowledgement 404, and a person without `alerts.manage` refused 403.
- The client template's screens test: the recipients saved on the Settings page, a failed deploy
  report, the health job run twice by the tick, the alert on the Alerts page as emailed with its
  message in email's table, "1 open" on Home, "I am on it" pressed, then a successful report and
  the alert resolved by the next run.
- `pnpm verify` with its twelve gates.

Not verified: the pages in a browser or on a phone, and a real email through a real provider.
