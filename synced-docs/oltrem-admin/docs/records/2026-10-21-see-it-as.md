# See it as

> **In plain words.** An Owner, or anyone allowed to read access, can now open a person and tap
> "See what they can do". The page lists everything in the portal, part by part, with a yes or a
> no for that person and the reason in plain words: their role gives it, a deny takes it away,
> nothing gives it, or they are the Owner. A row of places at the top shows the answer for each
> place. It only shows: nobody is signed in as the person, so nothing can be done in their name.

## What changed, and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **`GET /api/iam/people/:id/access?scopeId=`** under `iam.read`: every active capability with the check's answer and reason for that person at that place, the company when none is named | Plan 7.3: a read-only "See it as", never a real impersonated session; an Owner checks a grant or an exception before telling the person it works |
| **The page `/people/:id/see`** in `@oltrem/screens`, linked from the person page beside the exceptions | The answer is shown where the grants and exceptions are made |

## Decisions taken while building

- **The same check, not a copy of it.** The route compiles the person's access from the rows,
  as their next request would, and asks `can()` for each capability, so the page cannot disagree
  with what the person meets.
- **No session is made or touched.** Impersonation is treated as an escalation (the research,
  12): looking needs only `iam.read`, because it shows nothing the grants and exceptions do not
  already show.
- **One place at a time**, chosen from the row of places, as on the exceptions page.
- **An account switched off** answers with no items, and the page says it can do nothing.

## How it was verified

- The IAM routes test: a clerk at one place allowed there and not at the company, with the
  reasons `allow` and `not_granted`; the Owner's reason `owner`; an unknown place and an unknown
  person refused with 404; a clerk holding `iam.read` may look too.
- The template's screens test: the link on the person page; the deny and the allow with their
  reasons; the Owner's standing; an account switched off.
- `pnpm verify` with its twelve gates.

Not verified: the page in a browser or on a phone.
