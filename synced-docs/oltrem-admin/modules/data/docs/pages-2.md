# @oltrem/data: The pages: `/data`

> Part of [the @oltrem/data README](../README.md).

## The pages, continued

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `/data/connections/<id>/users`, `/storage`, `/functions`, `/logs`, `/health`, `/advisors`, `/settings` | A Supabase project's other sections, for someone with `data.insights.read`: since 2026-11-26 ten tabs under the head of each and of the database's page, each with its icon (its colour dot dropped the same day: one cue per tab) (Overview, Database, Users, Storage, Functions, Health, Advice, Usage, Logs, Settings; for two hours that day only the first five, until the owner found the options had moved and the row "blank and color less"). On the Database tab what you can do has its own line under the tabs, and after the tables, panes side by side: At a glance (the last check, the project's state, size, rows, row security, Postgres, region), each a row with its icon, its label and its value as a soft coloured pill; Editing, with Turn on editing; and for Supabase Look closer, a row for each of the project's other pages. Each page's summary line names the project once ("this Supabase project", or its own name when that does not say Supabase). Overview (`/overview`, the owner's ask of 2026-11-26): one verdict (All good, things to look at, or things to fix now), then Needs you, worst first, each problem with why it matters and where to fix it (`src/overview.ts`: the project not healthy, disk, memory or connections past 75% and 90%, public tables with row level security off, Supabase's urgent and suggested advice apart from that one, the newest backup failed or older than two days), the figures with what each means (memory, disk, connections, the database's size, the tables with how many are open to anyone), a part that could not be read said once, and Look closer, a card for each page under it with what it is for. Users: 25 a page, each on one line with their email or phone, how they sign in, when they joined and last signed in (UTC), Confirmed and Blocked, with Next page and First page. Invite someone for `data.edit` (Supabase sends its invite email), and each user's name opens their page (`?user=`): their details, and Send password reset, Mark email confirmed, Block or Let back in for `data.edit`, and Delete user for `data.delete`, each asking first and coming back saying it was done. Storage: since 2026-11-26 a file manager: New bucket for `data.manage` (its name, Public, the largest file, the only kinds of file), and with no bucket yet a line saying to press it; inside a bucket Upload files and New folder for `data.edit`, Bucket settings for `data.manage`, Empty bucket and Delete bucket for `data.delete`, each asking first; a file's name opens its page (`?bucket&file`): its size, type and when it changed, a look at an image, Open and Download (signed links), Share link (7 days, to copy), Rename or move, and Delete; each change sends its route below and comes back saying it was done. A line of figures (buckets, how many public, and with the access token the files and the space they take) and a card per bucket (amber for public, green for private) with tags for its files and size, Public or Private, the largest file it takes and the only kinds of file it takes, and for a manager Use in Files, which opens Files' storage form with Supabase Storage, the project, its region and the bucket filled in; then a bucket's folder with its path as links, folders first by name, each file's size, type and when it changed, 100 at a time with Next 100. Health: memory and disk used as shares of their size, connections of the most allowed, the database's size and the load of the last minute, a share past 75% marked to look at and past 90% failing, a figure Supabase does not give as a dash; then Requests (a column chart by service, Data API, Auth, Storage and Realtime, over the last hour, day or 7 days) and Backups (point-in-time recovery on or off, each daily copy with its state). Functions: the count, how many run and how many are open to anyone, then a card per Edge Function (its address, when it changed, Running, its version, Sign-in needed or Open to anyone) that opens its own page (`?fn=`, the owner: Functions "DOES NOTHING"): Run for `data.edit` (POST with a JSON body, or GET, sent once with the project's key; it runs for real, and its status, time and answer show at the top, a 401 explained), See the functions' logs and Code on Supabase; its state, calls, failed (with the share and how many were its own fault), typical and slowest time over the last hour, day or 7 days, a column chart of answered, refused and failed calls, and its details (address, who may call it, version, when it changed, where it starts, and the secrets it can read by name, never a value). Logs: a chip per log (API, Database, Auth, Storage, Functions, Realtime) and per stretch (last hour, last day), a search of the messages, the lines shown counted as errors, warnings and information (a level coloured only when it has lines), then the newest 100 lines with their level. Advisors: how many findings to fix now, look at or note, then a card each, Security first and then Speed, each group headed with its count: the tile in the level's colour, what, the detail (else the finding's name), its level and How to fix, Supabase's guide in a new tab. Four findings carry their fix in SQL on the card (`adviceFix` in `src/dialects/supabase.ts`: row level security off on a public table, a function without a fixed search path, a view that runs as its owner, a foreign key without an index), and for `data.manage` Fix it, which asks first with what the fix changes (an app reading the table with the public key stops seeing it until a policy allows it) and comes back saying Fixed. Settings: Services (each one Healthy, Starting or Not healthy), Sign-in (new sign-ups, email sign-in and whether a confirmation email is required, phone, guests, the authenticator app, the shortest password, the session length, captcha, who sends its emails, and the other ways in as tags), Security (SSL required, who may connect), in coloured panes (Sign-in on the left, Services, Security and Extensions stacked beside it), each pane saying how many rows want a look, each setting a row with its icon, its label and its value as a soft pill on the right, green for a protection on, amber for something open or weak (no confirmation email, guests, a password under 8, Supabase's built-in mail, no SSL, any IP address); the extensions turned on as tags under a heading with their count, a line naming any part the token may not read, and Change these on Supabase, marked as opening a new tab. Settings, Functions, Logs, Advisors, Requests and Backups need the connection's access token; without one each says how to add it (Health says it once) |
| `/data/connections/<id>/usage` | A Supabase project's allowance (the plan, compute and read-only state, then a card with a meter each for the database, disk, file storage, monthly active users and Edge Functions against their limits, then the plan's other limits and a link to Supabase's usage page for egress and the bill; see [the usage section](a-supabase-project-beyond-its-tables.md#a-databases-usage-neon-turso-and-upstash)), its tab last in the Supabase row. A Neon or Turso database's usage, for someone with `data.insights.read`, under a row of chips (Tables, Usage) on it and on the database's page. Neon: this period's compute time, active time, storage, data written and data sent out, the project, region, Postgres version and the period counted; the compute's state, size in compute units, when it sleeps and when it was last active; each branch as a card. Turso: this month's rows read and written, storage and bytes synced, the database's organization, plan, group and region, and delete protection; blocked reads or writes said first; the account's allowance, a card each for rows read and written, storage, bytes synced and databases with a meter against the plan's quota where Turso gives one (the whole account this billing cycle, as Turso counts its quotas), or one line when Turso did not give them. Upstash Redis: today's commands, reads, writes, keys, connections and hit rate; the plan's allowance as cards with a meter where the plan sets a limit (requests this month with its reads and writes, storage, and the month's cost against a set budget, shown only when there is a cost or a budget); the plan's other limits (commands a second, clients at once, largest request and value, memory); the database's plan, region, read copies, eviction and daily backups, and a link to the Upstash Console; a database Upstash says is not active is said first. Without the connection's API key it says how to add one |
| `/data/connections/<id>/changes/<change>` | One change: by whom, when, the row, each column before and after, whole (a deleted row's values), Open the row, and Undo change or Undo delete while it can be undone, or why not |

A connection made from the paste box or a card is checked at once, and the connection page opens
either way: "Connected" when the database answered, "Saved, but the database did not answer yet"
when it did not, with the reason under it. A new key says the same two ways. Check now pressed
again within 30 seconds answers the last check without calling the service. Nothing read from a
client's database is kept, each page being read when it is opened, except a change's values,
kept 30 days so it can be undone.
