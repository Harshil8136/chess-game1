# @oltrem/screens: The pages: `/`, `/search`, `/look`, `/notifications`, `/settings`, `/start`, `/setup`, `/accept` and more and more

> Part of [the @oltrem/screens README](../README.md).

## The pages

| Path | Shows | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | a Vendor with no company yet: the company form, its first Owner the one setup named as Owner (`GET /api/iam/setup-people`), the client file's address only when setup named none; anyone else: a band with a greeting for the time of day and the date (the frame script writes both in the person's clock), a line on what waits and how many parts need a look, and dials with rings to Needs you, Get started (while a step is open) and At a glance (`HomeWelcome`), after the first run's question while Set up for your business is an open step (`FirstRun` in `start.tsx`, to whoever may do it): "What kind of business do you run?", the kinds of business as chips, each opening `/start?kind=` with its modules ticked, and nothing saved until that page's button; every line of Needs you, Get started and that question opens the exact page and tab (Switch on backups at System's Settings, the alert recipients at `#settings-alerts` there, Invite a second person at `/people/invite`), which the client template's test opens one by one; then Jump back in, a row of chips to the pages opened last that the frame script fills from the browser (`HomeRecent`, hidden while empty); then, side by side from a page 60rem wide, Needs you (what waits for them from each module that answers it, each a row to the page that acts on it, or a line saying nothing waits) over Get started, with a bar of the steps done, while a step of setting the portal up is open (each step ticked by what is stored, an open one a row to its page, or with a button when one press does it, `POST /do/get-started`; with email on, the email step leads to the one-step setup, `/email/connect`, and comes first, so the invitations of the step after it can be emailed; the Features page can switch the card off, and its Hide does the same for the Vendor and Owners), What changed (an extra that starts off, `audit.since`): for whoever may read the audit log, what others changed since they last pressed Mark as seen (a day back before the first press, a week back at most), the kinds of change the most made first, each a row to the audit log of its module, with "N other changes" for the rest, or "Nobody else changed anything."; Mark as seen (`POST /do/seen`) moves the mark, and showing the card never does, and At a glance: the Home figure of each mounted module that has one and that they may read, one row each with lined-up columns (tile, name and line, figure, state), failing and needing a look first, work switched off last as a quiet Off that the band does not count | `/api/iam/me` (and `/api/iam/setup-people` for the company form), then each `/api/<module>/onboarding`, `/api/<module>/todo` and `/api/<module>/home`, together, with `/api/users/me/seen` then `/api/audit/since?at=` while What changed is on |
| every module page, `/records`, `/forms` | before the company exists: the company form, as on `/` (the Vendor's) or "This portal has no company yet" (anyone else), never the page's own refusal; the portal's own pages (Status, Settings, Look, Notifications, the delivery log, Email services) still open as themselves | `/api/iam/me`, `GET /api/iam/setup-people` |
| `/search?as=json&q=` | no page: the JSON the palette shows under its pages as someone types (after a 250 ms pause), for two letters or more: the sections by name, line and `keywords`, the settings by title and line (to the Settings page searched for it), the roles and the locations by name, then each module that searches, as the person (a module that refuses them is left out). The palette also lists each `NAV` entry's `actions` (what the page starts: New role, Invite a user) under Actions, five before a word is typed, and any page opens one of its dialogs from `?open=<id>`, once, the word then dropped from the address | each `/api/<module>/search` |
| `/look` | Look and colours: the 24 palettes of `looks.ts` (a primary, an accent and greys leaning to its hue, both themes) as tiles showing their gradient, and the backgrounds (Soft wash, Plain, Aurora, Sunrise, Dots, Grid, Lines) as tiles showing a piece of each (`preview`); a press saves at once (`data-autosave`), Save only for a page without its script, Back to the portal's own. An Owner or the Vendor also has Make it everyone's look: `POST /do/look/everyone` saves the look on the form as the `settings.look` setting through `PUT /api/settings/values/settings.look` and clears their own cookie (a refusal shows as a toast). `POST /do/look` keeps `<palette>.<pattern>` in the `oltrem_look` cookie (an id not in the lists is the kit's own; reset clears it); every page then carries the look's CSS in `<style id="ui-look">` in its head, about a kilobyte, none for the kit's own look; the frame's script swaps it with the page. `createScreens({ look })` is the portal's look until a person picks one: a string, or a function read on each page (the template's returns `settings.look`, else the client file's `brand.look`) | `/api/iam/me`; `PUT /api/settings/values/settings.look` |
| `/notifications?show=&before=` | the person's notifications, All or Unread, Mark all read, thirty a page, and how each module tells them; `/notifications/:id` marks one read and opens its page; Keep portal emails in your main inbox: `/notifications/gmail-filter.xml`, `/notifications/portal-contact.vcf`, `POST /notifications/primary-test` | `/api/users/me/notifications`, `/api/users/me/notifications/choices`, `GET /api/email/primary`, `POST /api/email/primary/test` |
| `/settings/section/:id?show=changed&q=` | a section's own Settings tab (`section-settings.tsx`), last in the row of every section whose parts define a setting the Settings page does not keep (Users and access, Sales, Files, Email, System and so on, the id the section's name in lower case with dashes): its parts' options (their extras as switches) at the top, then its settings in a card per part (since 2026-11-24, like the Options card above them: headed with the part's name, or Settings when there is one part), in the same rows, Save bar, Changed, search and one value for all as the Settings page, without the figures and the jump list when it holds fewer than eight; then **Set up**, the pages opened under it (`under`): Email's Setup, Addresses, Mail setup and Forwarding, Users and access's Access reviews and Vendor access. Every form on it sends `to`, so its answer comes back to the tab. The tab shows to an Owner and the Vendor, and to anyone who may open a page set up under it; the API still decides each row | `/api/settings/values`, `/api/settings/parts` |
| `/settings/parts?show=&q=&preset=` | whoever holds `settings.parts` (every Owner and the Vendor at first, anyone a role gives it to; the tab shows to them alone, asked of the API when the Settings tabs are on screen, and anyone else at the address reads "You need the permission to switch modules"): the Features page, a dashboard. All, On (n) and Off (n) chips and a search box (at once with the frame script, by `?q=` without it); the presets as "Start from" chips (Everything, Email only, Nothing optional, and the set saved now) that fill the boxes without saving; a one-line note; then a section per sidebar group (Work, Company, Users and access, Messages, System), each one even list whose rows line up their columns on a wide screen: the sidebar's icon, the name, one line, what it needs and what needs it, and a checkbox (Always on for the core), the icon grey while unticked, and, while it is unticked, a line that the modules needing it go off with it (kept in step by the frame script before Save; Save switches them off in the same save and the page names them at the top, `?also=`, where before it was refused until they were unticked by hand); under a module's line, its optional extras (the daily audit seal, the emails about access ending, the review and restore-drill reminders), each a row with its own box under the module, a core module's too, kept as they are saved when a preset fills the module boxes; one Save bar at the bottom sends the whole set | `/api/settings/parts`, `PUT /api/settings/parts` |
| `/start?kind=&done=` | Set up for your business, a tab of the Settings set beside Features and the first step of Get started, to whoever holds `settings.parts` (anyone else reads that they need the permission): the kinds of business as chips in a sideways row (Services by appointment, Shop, Office, Field team, Something else); the kind picked, with "Which of these do you use?": every module that can be switched off as a ticked chip in a sideways row, the ones the kind uses ticked (what is on now for Something else), a ticked one keeping on what it needs, the unticked ones switched off with their data kept; then what one press also does in words (the roles Admin and Member, Admin ranked above, or that the roles are left to whoever manages them when the person cannot see them, the starter list with its columns, each "there already" when it is) and "Set up the portal for this" ("Run it again" for the kind saved, "Save what I use" for Something else); then the next steps: Invite your team, Add your locations, Bring in your customers, Fill in your lists. The press saves the modules and the kind in one save, then makes what is missing (a role by name, the list, Records included when this press switched it on): pressing again makes nothing twice and deletes nothing, a step that fails is named while the others stay done, and one press stays under 30 database queries (locations, about ten each, are added on the Locations page) | `GET /api/settings/parts`, `PUT /api/settings/parts`, `/api/iam/roles`, `/api/iam/capabilities`, `/api/records/lists` |
| `/setup/company` | the invitation link for the first Owner, with Copy, after the form is sent; a refusal keeps the name and address typed and says why and what to do (`companyRefusal`: giving the Vendor's own address says the first Owner is the client, 2026-11-15) | `POST /api/iam/tenant` |
| `/accept?token=` | the invitation and its Accept button; accepting returns to `/?done=joined`, where Home says "You joined <the portal's name>." A refusal shows the problem's title, its detail and its next step; an invitation that cannot be used (410) or one for another address offers no Accept, and every invitation page, like the no-account page, has a way to sign in with another address: the local door's sign-out on this computer, Cloudflare Access's `/cdn-cgi/access/logout` behind Access (`SignOutToSwitch`) | `POST /api/iam/invitations/accept` |
| `/records` | the lists the person may see, with their records and location; Create list when a role allows it somewhere | `/api/records/lists` |
| `/records/new` | a new list: its name, its location picked from a row, and a starting shape (blank, contacts, tasks, stock, orders, equipment) | `/api/records/lists` |
| `/records/:id?q=&field=&value=&sort=&dir=&page=` | a list's records: searched, narrowed by a row of chips per choice or yes or no field, sorted from a row, paged; the add form and Add from a spreadsheet when the person may add | `/api/records/lists/:id`, `items` |
| `/records/:id/items/:item` | one record with who added and changed it; the form to change it and Remove, as the person may | `/api/records/lists/:id`, `items/:item` |
| `/records/:id/fields` | the designer: the fields in order with up, down and Remove; Add a field; the name and location; Remove the list once empty | `/api/records/lists/:id`, `/api/records/lists` |
| `/forms` | the forms the person may see, with their new answers and whether each takes answers; Make a form when a role allows it somewhere | `/api/forms/forms` |
| `/forms/new` | a new form: its name, its location picked from a row, and a starting shape (contact us, quote request, feedback, sign-up, blank) | `/api/forms/forms` |
| `/forms/:id?view=&mine=&q=&page=` | the inbox: a row of statuses with their counts (to do, new, open, done, all, and Unread); an answer nobody has opened yet in bold, read aloud as unread, a row for the answers the person took, a search box, paged; Try the form, the designer, the download | `/api/forms/forms/:id`, `entries` |
| `/forms/:id/entries/:entry` | one answer as it was sent; Take it or Let it go, Mark new, open or done; its notes and email replies, and the form that adds one; Remove, as the person may; shown, the page marks the answer read for the team through the frame's `data-seen`, and Mark unread puts it back | `/api/forms/forms/:id`, `entries/:entry`, `entries/:entry/read` |
| `/forms/:id/design` | the designer: the code for a website, a new address, the fields with up, down and Remove, Add a field, the settings (name, line, taking answers, the page after sending, who is told, location), Remove | `/api/forms/forms/:id`, `/api/forms/forms` |
| `/forms/:id/try` | the form as a website shows it, posting to its public address | `/api/forms/forms/:id`, `/hooks/forms/:key` |
