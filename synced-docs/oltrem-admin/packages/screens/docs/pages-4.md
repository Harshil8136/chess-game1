# @oltrem/screens: The pages: `/settings`, `/alerts`, `/audit`

> Part of [the @oltrem/screens README](../README.md).

## The pages, continued

| Path | Shows | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| `/settings?show=changed&q=` | since 2026-11-22 only what is company-wide: Name and icon, Colours for everyone, the Settings module's own rows and Security (who may get in and how), then **Each section's settings**, a row for each section's own Settings tab this person may open with how many settings it holds; every other setting lives on its section's tab (`/settings/section/:id`, below). Before that: the settings in sections with plain names, in a fixed order (Company, Email, Money, Users and access, Security; System for the Vendor alone), each a chip in a sideways row and an anchor; All and Changed (n) chips and a search box (at once with the frame script, by `?q=` without it). Each row: its title, one line, its value in a control of its type (a box, a number with its unit, a switch with On or Off beside it as it stands); since Look 3 one Save bar rises at the foot once anything changed, with Discard, instead of a Save on every row (always showing without the page script); Changed with the default and Reset to default when it is not the default, and who changed it last and when. An Owner, or anyone but the Vendor, sees only the rows they may change, and one line counting the rest; the Vendor also sees each key, small and muted. Settings that share a name across modules (the currencies, the time zones) get one form above them that sets them all. Since 2026-11-11: the page opens with its figures (settings, changed, sections, after a deploy); each section has its icon and colour (the sidebar's group colours) and a line on what it holds, with its count and how many are changed; on a wide screen the sections are a list down the left that stays in view, on a phone a sideways row of the same chips with icons; a changed row is marked along its left edge, and its default and last change are small tags; whoever may change `settings.look` (an Owner, the Vendor) gets **Colours for everyone** first: the 24 palettes and the 7 backgrounds of `looks.ts` as tiles in two sideways rows, saved with the page's one form; and **Name and icon** for an Owner and the Vendor: the portal's name (up to 40 letters, empty for the client file's) and its tile as a sideways row: Initials, the 32 icons of `MARK_ICONS` (Lucide, each named for what it draws) and Picture, with Upload a picture. The frame script draws the picked file on a 128 px canvas and sends it as WebP (PNG where the browser cannot) in `brand:picture`; `POST /settings` takes it only as WebP, PNG or JPEG read from its own bytes, at most 48 KB (`pictureOf`), keeps it in the bucket under the first 16 hex digits of its SHA-256 and saves `picture:<file>`; `brand:name` and `brand:mark` save `settings.portal_name` and `settings.portal_mark` with the rest of the form | `/api/settings/values` |
| `/alerts` | the open alerts with what became of their email and who is on each, then the ones resolved lately | `/api/alerts/items` |
| `/audit?q=&range=&who=&module=&outcome=&actor=&target=&day=&ip=&asn=&key=&session=&signal=&before=` | the Audit log, remade 2026-11-24, the way GitHub's and Google Workspace's logs read: a search over people, records, addresses and actions beside Export; chip rows for When (24 hours, 7, 30 or 90 days; 30 by default), Everyone, People, Automatic or API keys, the result and the section; one line with how many entries and how many refused, the tamper check and the time zone; the signals: Refused, API key calls, Downloads and New places, each a count in the range and a tap to its rows; from 7 days up, a strip of one column a day coloured by result, a tap listing that day alone (`day=`) while the strip keeps the range; then the rows under their days in the viewer's own clock (the frame script keeps the zone in a cookie), each one sentence ("Ana changed the role Front desk", `audit-words.ts`), with the section in its sidebar colour, the browser in a few words and the country under it, a refusal's reason in red, an unusual country or new browser on a sign-in in amber, and a red (refused) or amber (failed) edge down the row; the same thing done again right after folds into one row ("3 times"); a call made with an API key says so, with a key in its picture, and a file handed over is named under its row; a row opens to What changed (field, before muted, after in green; a value the log does not keep as "Not kept"), why it was refused (its own words, error code, the fields), who and how (the API key, the sign-in), where from (address, place, network and its number, browser, the agent as sent), how (request, answer, time taken, file and size, calls held back), its fingerprint, its other facts and the entry itself, with links to everything that person did, in that sign-in, with that API key, to that record, from that address and from that network; Older pages back; the daily seals and the export form | `/api/audit/entries` with `since` (or a day's `since` and `until`), `count=1`, `offset` and `countSince`, `seals` |
