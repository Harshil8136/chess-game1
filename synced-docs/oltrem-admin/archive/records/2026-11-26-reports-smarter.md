# Reports: smarter readings

**Changed.** The trend on Activity marks what happened: campaigns that went out, invoices sent
(from the counts) and each new version of the portal that went live (from the deploy reports).
An unusual day, half or more away from the average of the same weekday in the four weeks before,
comes first in What stands out and shows under each website app; a new extra, **Unusual days**
(`analytics.unusual`, off on every portal, migration 0103), says it on Alerts each morning and
clears it on a usual day. The Monday summary adds each Money amount against the week before, one
line from visit to paid and each website app's visitors. **Custom** picks any 1 to 90 days of the
last year, each figure against as many days just before them. **Why.** The owner: "data
collecting is endless"; batch 6 of the plan.

**Before → after.** A trend without context, the last 7, 30 or 90 days only, a summary of counts →
the days that explain a bump, any days, a warning when a day is far from usual, money and visitors
in the Monday email. No new table; the summary reads each section in-process as the first Owner,
after the same capability check its route makes.

**Checked.** `pnpm verify`; the unusual reading on its own; a range with its marks on D1; the
summary's lines read once as an Owner; the morning check raising and clearing its alert; the page's
marks, unusual lines and Custom form. Phone tests P988-P989, P1000-P1002. Not checked: a real phone,
a provider's live website figures, a real Monday.
