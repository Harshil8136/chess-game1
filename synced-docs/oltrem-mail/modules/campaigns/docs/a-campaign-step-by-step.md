# @oltrem/campaigns: A campaign, step by step: Draft, The list, From, Merge fields, The message, A test, Template, Saved list and more and more

> Part of [the @oltrem/campaigns README](../README.md).

## A campaign, step by step

| Stage                 | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Draft                 | A name and a place; then a subject and a message in plain text with merge fields, and a list. Only a draft is changed or deleted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| The list              | A CSV file of 1 MB at most a request (read only that far, even when its length is not declared: `readCapped`, 2026-11-20), saved by any spreadsheet: a heading row, an `email` column (`email_address`, `e_mail`, `mail` and `correo` are read too; also any heading that says email or correo, or the column whose values are mostly addresses), a comma or a semicolon between fields. The page reads a larger file on the phone or computer and sends it in parts of about 600,000 characters, the first replacing the list and the rest added, saying which part goes (`data-csv` of the kit's frame script); the first part carries `total`, the people of the whole file, so a file longer than `campaigns.max_recipients` allows is refused before any part is taken; past 30 columns the address, the name, the files and the columns the message needs are kept first and the rest named back. **Download a sample CSV** (`GET /campaigns/:id/sample`) gives the email and name columns and one for each field the message names, with three example people at example.com; the same headings with no rows come from **Blank template** (`?blank=1`), and the campaigns page offers a **Blank CSV template** before any campaign exists (`GET /template`: `email`, `name`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `phone`, `company`). On the page the file is chosen in a drop zone (drop a file on it, or tap to choose one) with both downloads under it. Every other column becomes a merge field named from its heading (`First name` is `first_name`); a `name` column, or `first_name` and `last_name`, is the person's name. Addresses are checked and lowered; a likely misspelling (`gmial.com`, `.con`) is put right and named, unless the person ticks **Keep the addresses exactly as written** (`fix=0`); a repeated one is counted and left out; a bad row is reported by its number; a no-reply address is counted, and skipped when it sends. A draft nobody is on yet takes its first file whole; any later file is added to who is on it. The setting `campaigns.max_recipients` caps it. The file is read by `readList` of `@oltrem/email` (moved there on 2026-10-28, so saved lists read people the same way). |
| From                  | One of the sender addresses the person may use for bulk mail, its domain passing its check, picked on the page; the domain's default for bulk comes first. It may be changed until the campaign is sent or cancelled, by a writer while it is a draft and by a sender after. An address that also sends everyday email (its purpose is both) is said to on the page, since 2026-11-12: Gmail learns an address from its campaigns and files its everyday email under Promotions too, so the page asks for an address only for campaigns. |
| Merge fields          | `{{first_name}}` is the person's value; `{{first_name                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | there}}`uses`there`when it is empty.`{{email}}`and`{{name}}` always exist. A field the list does not have keeps the campaign from starting. |
| The message           | The text as written, with the footer and an unsubscribe link under it; an HTML version of the same text, every value escaped, paragraphs at blank lines and web addresses made links. The page previews it as the first person of the list will receive it. The HTML is a plain letter, as a person's own mail app sends one and as Write does (`compose`): no coloured page, no card, the footer a small grey line under the words with the unsubscribe link; a designed card is one of the signs Gmail files an email under Promotions by. The one-click unsubscribe headers stay, as the law and Gmail's bulk-sender rules ask. Over the preview the page says which of Gmail's tabs it will most likely reach (`tabSigns`, the view's `promotion`): with a sign of a promotion (the signs Write reads in the subject and the words, shouting among them; a design in place of a plain letter; a From name that says newsletter) it names each, what to change, and Compose for a few people; with none it says it reads like a letter. It promises nothing: Gmail decides for each person.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| A test                | Sent to the signed-in person's own address, as the first person would receive it, its subject marked `[Test]`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Template              | Instead of plain text, a saved template of the email module (Email, Templates) made for campaigns, which the person may read; one made for the Write page alone is refused. A draft sends the template's version current when it starts; the version is fixed from then on. |
| Saved list            | Instead of a file, a saved list of the email module (Campaigns, Lists), its people ranked by address. Since 2026-11-14 **Send email** on a list's page opens Campaigns at `/campaigns?list=<id>`: the draft is named (the list's name and the day) and made with that list, and opens on its message step; a list that could not be added is said on the draft's who step, with the reason. A saved list is added to who is on it; only a draft nobody is on yet takes it whole, and keeps its name for **Only “the list's name”** below. |
| Adding people         | The who step's **Add people** offers four ways as cards in their own colours, as a list's page does: **Saved list** (`use-list`), **Type addresses** (one a line or parted by commas, a name optional as `Ana López <ana@example.com>`: `POST /campaigns/:id/list/add?typed=1`, read by the email module's `typedCsv`), **Upload a file** (the drop zone above) and **From your email** (the people the signed-in person emailed from Write, most emailed first, without the ones already on the campaign: `GET /campaigns/:id/suggestions`, through the email module's `emailed`; ticked, filtered and posted as typed addresses). Typed and picked people need **I confirm these people agreed to receive email from us**, sent as `confirm=1`: the API refuses typed people without it (a saved list keeps the reason given at its own import). Who is on it stays, the same address is added once and whoever is never mailed is skipped. **Add people** is also a command on every other step of a draft and on a running campaign, and **Take people off** is offered on a draft with people too. |
| Files                 | PDF, Office, picture, text or ZIP files a draft sends, kept by the email module in the client's bucket: up to 1,000 of 1 GB together, 10 MB attached in each email; a file over 10 MB, up to 95 MB, is a link to download at the end of the email that works for 30 days, and needs no provider that takes files. With the script, the files chosen go straight to the campaign's route, several at once. A file is for **everyone**, or for **each person**: it goes to the one person whose address its name carries (`Invoice ann@example.com (October).pdf`), or to whoever's list row names it in a `file`, `files`, `attachment`, `attachments` or `archivo` column (several split by `;`). While the campaign has a file for each person, who has none of their own is skipped with why, and the page counts them before it starts. Each email is then its own call, through Brevo or Resend (which download each file from a link that lasts a day); a sender on another provider waits and says so. Only a draft takes or loses files; deleting it removes them. |
| Approval              | A send to more people than `campaigns.approval_over` (1,000 by default; 0 for none) is not started but asked for: an alert says so, and someone else who may approve large sends says yes (it starts at once, or at its time) or no with a note (it stays a draft, the note on its page). A change to the message, the sender or the list withdraws the ask. An Owner or the Vendor who holds `campaigns.approve` there, when no other active person of the company may approve campaigns at the campaign's place (each compiled as their next request would be, nothing recorded; the Vendor is not counted; a company of more than 50 other people, `PEOPLE_CHECKED`, is taken to have someone), gives their own yes: their start sends at once, the campaign is marked approved by them with a note saying why, `start` answers `approvedAlone: true` and its audit row carries `approvedAlone`; an ask made earlier is answered by its asker on the page once nobody else can. The view's `approval.alone` says so, and the page offers **Send it** with that said in its dialog. |
| Pace                  | Its own messages a run (1 to 1,000), its own cap a day, and its sending hours (from, to, the days) in the time zone the client hands in (`timeZone`; the kit's client template gives the company's clock, `bookings.time_zone`, where it gave the alerts' quiet-hours zone); the view carries it (`timeZone`) and the page names it under the hours instead of pointing at a settings page; an end before the start runs overnight. Empty uses the settings. Changed while it runs too. |
| Start                 | Now, or in one, three or 24 hours (any time within 90 days through the API). It needs a subject, a message, someone to send to, merge fields the list has (and, a default value for a field any of them has empty), the footer setting and a sender address on a passing domain; what is missing is named on the page. The portal's address at that moment is where the unsubscribe links point. Until a campaign started for later sends to anyone, **Back to draft to change it** (`campaigns.send`) makes it a draft again: everything may be changed, and it is started (and approved, if needed) again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
