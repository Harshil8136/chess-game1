# Backlinks & Citations Playbook

Owner-facing playbook for off-page SEO — the one lever that cannot be coded.
Backlinks (links from other sites to madagascarhotelags.com) and brand
mentions are the strongest remaining ranking input for this site: they feed
classic Google rankings, the local pack, and AI-engine citations (2026
research: consistent third-party brand mentions correlate with being cited
by ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews even more strongly than raw link counts).

Companion to [SEO-OPERATIONS.md](./SEO-OPERATIONS.md) §5–6. Re-check
quarterly.

---

## 1. Goals & KPIs

- **2–3 earned links or listings per quarter** (quality over volume — one
  local vet or newspaper link beats fifty directory spam links).
- Track monthly: referring domains (Bing Webmaster Tools → Backlinks, free),
  local-pack position for "hotel para perros aguascalientes" / "pensión
  canina aguascalientes", and GSC branded-query impressions.
- Never buy links, join link farms, or exchange reciprocal-link pages —
  Google penalizes both sides.

### Tier 2 — partnerships (ongoing, highest local value)

- **Veterinarias** near both branches (Héroes / Jardines del Sol):
  cross-referral agreement + "hospedaje recomendado" mention on their
  site/Facebook. Offer the same back from the blog.
- **Asociaciones protectoras / rescates de Aguascalientes**: sponsor or host
  an adoption day — generates press, social mentions and links naturally.
- **Groomers, pet shops, entrenadores** that don't offer boarding:
  complementary-service referral exchange.

- **LJA.mx** and **El Heraldo de Aguascalientes**: pitch listicle/seasonal
  angles — "mejores hoteles para perros en Aguascalientes", "qué hacer con
  tu mascota en vacaciones", Semana Santa / verano / diciembre.
- **Feria de San Marcos**: pet-friendly visitor roundups — the site's
  travel-with-pets blog post is the natural citation.
- Local podcasts/YouTube (Aguascalientes lifestyle) — 30 years of history
  (since 1994) is a story hook.

## 8. Beyond Aguascalientes (phase 5 of ADR-0004, prepared 2026-10-29)

The texts the owner sends or posts to be found outside the state, under the rules of
[ADR-0004](./adr/0004-visibility-beyond-aguascalientes.md): the same name, address and phone
everywhere (§2), and nothing that claims a branch, a service area or a town the hotel does not
serve. Every fact below comes from `src/data/canonical-facts.ts` and the service pages; check
them again before sending if months have passed. The owner approves each pitch and listing
before it goes out (SEO-OPERATIONS §10).

### 8c. Video: three short scripts

Vertical, 30 to 45 seconds, filmed at the hotels with real pets (with their owners'
permission). Post each to TikTok (`@hotelmadagascar`), Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and the
Google Business Profile of the hotel it shows. Each says only what the fact sheet says.

1. **"Tu gato nunca escucha ladridos"** (Hotel Felino). Shots: the entrance, a multi-level
   room, the solarium, a cat resting. Voice: "En Aguascalientes tenemos un hotel solo para
   gatos, aparte del de perros. Cada gato tiene su habitación, sin jaulas, con solárium y
   veterinario de guardia 24/7. Desde $286 por día." Caption ends with the booking link.
2. **"Así se cobra: por día"**. Shots: a calendar, a dog arriving, the same dog leaving. Voice:
   "Cobramos por día: el día de entrada y el de salida cuentan. Si llega el lunes y sale el
   martes, son dos días. Sin cargos por hora." Then the three weight prices, $286, $390 and
   $490.
3. **"Qué incluye su estancia"**. Shots: a meal, a walk, the cameras, a staff member sending a
   video. Voice: the eight items of `BOARDING_INCLUDED`, one per shot.

### 8d. Wikidata: not yet

Wikidata accepts an item for an entity "that can be described using serious and publicly
available references" ([Wikidata:Notability](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Notability),
criterion 2). The hotel's own website and directory listings are not such references, and an
item a business makes about itself without them is likely to be deleted. Create one only after
§8b brings coverage in regional or national media, and cite that coverage in it.
